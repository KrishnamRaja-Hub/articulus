- `./run_all.sh` runs every engine and data suite. Each prints `<suite>: N pass, M fail`, and failures print as `FAIL <id> <detail>`.

## Notable passes (guarantees confirmed)

- `npm test` passes 13 of 13, and `scripts/smoke.mjs` passes 95 of 95.
- **Data:** 5,611 course groups in total, and none spans two colleges (the README claim holds). Every group course is in `catalog`. Every catalog key matches `institutionId:PREFIX NUMBER`, every unit value is above 0 and finite, every institution id is known, every N_OF `n` is in range, there are no empty nodes, no duplicate groups inside a requirement, and every requirement with no groups carries a `noArticulation` reason.
- **The solver never plans a course at a college outside `allowed`**: 0 of 1,650 matrix solves and 0 of 2,640 fuzz solves. It never throws, including with `allowed: []`, a home college not in `allowed`, garbage ids, `unitCap` 0 or negative, and more than `maxTerms` terms (courses are never dropped).
- With no courses taken, the solver produced **no split violations** in any of the 1,650 matrix plans. `plan.result.isValid` is true exactly when `unsolvable` is empty in every matrix run. Per-term unit totals always equal the sum of the converted course units, no term exceeds the cap unless it holds a single course, and no course is planned twice.
- The same inputs always give the same plan, regardless of the order of `allowed` or of the taken set (0 of 1,650 and 0 of 2,640 differed).
- verify: Scenario 2 is detected, duplicates of the same code at two colleges are not flagged, a 3-college split lists 3 partials, a requirement completed at a third college (Irvine Valley 4B alone) is not flagged, the sanctioned De Anza MATH 1B + MATH 1CH mix works, and a requirement that appears twice in the tree is reported once.
- solve: Scenario 2 is repaired at De Anza, at Foothill only, and at a third college. Taken courses and their honors twins are not planned again. A group already complete at a non-allowed college is accepted. N_OF shortfalls are reported in `unsolvable`.
- Term names are correct for quarter starts in Fall, Winter and Spring and for semester starts in Fall and Spring across year boundaries. Conversion is correct: 3 semester units = 4.5 quarter units, 4 semester units = 6.
- normalize: a multi-cell row becomes OR, a group "Or" conjunction becomes OR, sending "Or" groups fan out, no-articulation rows (explicit or missing) keep a reason, merges de-duplicate groups, series cells work, whitespace is trimmed, and the template-mismatch warning fires.
- UI: all 22 majors load with matching badge labels. Rapid switching between majors and between universities settles on the last selection (the `load.current` guard works). Search tolerates `phys4b`, extra spaces and `4b`, and special, regex or HTML characters, emoji and 500-character input cause no crash. Enter and Escape work, and taken courses are hidden from results. The Mission-only "not articulated at the selected colleges" label shows. There are no console errors in production, no React key or other warnings in dev, and no page-level horizontal scroll at 390px.

## Questionable by design

1. **A split in an optional course or unused alternative is fatal and cannot be repaired** (V12–V15, U28). The README says both "Optional (recommended) subtrees … never fail their parent" and "Split-series violations are always fatal, even if the tree is satisfiable another way." The code follows the second rule. Example: a full De Anza plan plus Pasadena ENGR 011 and Irvine Valley ENGR 30 (the *recommended* MEC ENG C85) gives `isValid: false`. The solver skips optional and unchosen branches (`solve.ts:65`), so it never repairs this. The plan stays invalid with 0 terms left, and the UI violation card has no "Repair" line. A UC would simply ignore those courses.
2. **20 of 22 agreements are "unsolvable" in essentially every setup.** 1,407 of 1,650 matrix runs report requirements with 0 articulations anywhere, such as UCLA COM SCI 35L and UCSD CSE 29, as unmet. ASSIST lists these as "No Course Articulated" for courses usually taken after transfer. Only Berkeley CS B.A. and EECS ever show the green badge, so "100% articulation integrity" (Plan.md's promised badge) is effectively unreachable for most majors.
3. **Double counting.** In Berkeley ME, `CHEM 1A, CHEM 1AL, CHEM 1B` appears both as a required row and as an option in the "choose 1" science list. One chemistry series satisfies both (V17 confirms it), so the solver never plans a second science course. Whether ASSIST allows this cannot be checked without the raw template.
4. **Minimal units.** Plan.md asks for a "minimal-unit course set". The greedy solver picks 3+3 units where one 5-unit course covers both requirements (S01). The README admits this. F-10 is the easily fixable part of it.
5. **Taken courses stay when the university or major changes** (U20). They carry into the next agreement and can produce violations there. This is sensible for one student, but courses that are not in the new catalog stay as chips and silently count for nothing.
6. **Ties across colleges are decided by the data's group order, not by the order of the `allowed` list** (F-11). The app is deterministic only because the fixture order is fixed. Regenerating the fixtures with `npm run fetch` could change which college a plan sends the student to, with no change in units.
